Privacy Policy

Don't Press Me (iOS and Android)

Last updated: 16 September 2026
Developer: Ali Sarsur
Contact: alisarsur1306@gmail.com
The short version. Don't Press Me has no accounts, no login, and no server of its own. Your scores, settings and play statistics are stored locally and are not automatically uploaded to us. If you choose to share a score, you choose the destination. Advertising and payment services collect the information described below.

What stays on your device

The game stores the following in your device's local storage. We do not automatically upload these records to a server of our own:

  • Your high score and best streak.
  • Learned rules and whether you completed or skipped the introduction.
  • Local counts of introduction opens/completions/skips, run starts/finishes/retries, and attempts/misses by rule. Clear these in Settings without clearing scores, purchases or learned rules.
  • Your settings: language, sound on/off, haptics on/off.
  • What you have purchased, and how many continues you have left.

There is no cloud save and no way for us to see any of it.

What leaves your device

Score sharing — only when you choose

Challenge a friend offers your score, level and App Store link to the system share sheet. You choose whether to send it and the destination. On supported browsers without a share sheet, the same text can be copied to the clipboard. Play statistics and purchase records are not included.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The free version shows banner and interstitial ads, and offers an optional rewarded video ad in exchange for extra lives. These are served by Google AdMob, which collects:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android).
  • Device and app information: model, operating system version, app version, coarse region and language.
  • Whether an ad was shown, viewed, or tapped.

Google uses this to select ads, to measure them, and to detect fraud. Google's own policy governs what it does with the data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On iOS, the app asks for permission before any of this is used to track you across other companies' apps. Declining is fine: you still get ads, they are just less targeted. You can change the answer later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Buying "Remove Ads" stops this entirely: no banners, no interstitials, and no ad requests.

Purchases: RevenueCat, Apple, and Google

If you buy something, the payment is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details, name, or billing address.

To confirm a purchase is genuine and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-specific user ID, the store receipt, and the product you bought. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is not, however, directed at children under 13, because it carries third-party advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ad networks, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Your rights

Because there is no account and no server, we hold nothing about you to look up, export, or delete. To remove everything associated with the app:

  • Delete your local data: uninstall the app.
  • Reset advertising data: reset your advertising identifier in your device settings, as described above.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: decline the tracking prompt on iOS, or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Requests about data held by Google or RevenueCat: go to them directly, at the policy links above. We can point you to the right form.

If you are in the EU or UK, the legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the settings above; the legal basis for processing a purchase is performance of our contract with you.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date changes and the new version is posted at the same address. Continuing to use the app after that means you accept the updated policy.
