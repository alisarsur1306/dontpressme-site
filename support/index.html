Don't Press Me: Support

Questions, bugs, or a purchase that didn't go through? Email alisarsur1306@gmail.com and include your phone model and what happened.

My purchase isn't showing

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

I still see ads after buying Remove Ads

Restore purchases from the Shop, then close and reopen the game. Watching a rewarded ad for extra lives is always optional.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned on or off in Settings.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. There are no accounts and nothing to delete on our side.
